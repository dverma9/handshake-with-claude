📋 How to Play

Your mission: Manage a global supply chain for 3 minutes. Alerts will appear — triage and resolve them to protect KPIs.

Priority Levels

  • 🔴 Critical — resolve immediately or suffer severe KPI damage
  • 🟠 High — resolve quickly, costs mount over time
  • 🔵 Medium — manageable, but don't ignore too long

KPIs

  • Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transportation Efficiency
  • Operating Cost (lower is better), Revenue Protected, Score

Actions

  • Green buttons = best action for this alert
  • Red buttons = risky choice, may backfire
  • Ignoring or delaying always costs you

Scoring

  • Best action: +150–300 pts + KPI boost
  • Acceptable action: +50–100 pts, minor KPI hit
  • Ignore / Delay: KPI damage, cost spike
