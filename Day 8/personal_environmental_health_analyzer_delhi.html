🏆 Top 3 Cleanest Cities

1
Bangalore — AQI 45 (Good): India's tech capital enjoys the cleanest air among major metros, aided by elevation, tree cover, and proximity to the Western Ghats. PM2.5 at ~14 µg/m³ is near WHO interim targets.
2
Mumbai — AQI 63 (Moderate): Sea breezes from the Arabian Sea naturally disperse pollutants. Active monsoon onset in June 2026 is helping clear the air further.
3
Chennai — AQI 66 (Moderate): Coastal location and Bay of Bengal winds keep particulate levels relatively low despite heavy traffic and industrial activity.

⚠️ Top 3 Most Polluted Cities

1
Kolkata — AQI 133 (Poor): Highest polluted major metro right now. Industrial emissions, vehicular traffic, and high humidity (84%) trapping pollutants. PM2.5 at ~58 µg/m³ — nearly 6× WHO's safe limit.
2
Delhi — AQI 82 (Moderate): Despite being summer (typically better months), Delhi's AQI still exceeds WHO safe levels. PM10 at 176 µg/m³ suggests heavy dust and construction particulates.
3
Hyderabad — AQI 82 (Moderate): Tied with Delhi, Hyderabad faces growing urban pollution from rapid IT-led expansion and construction activity.

💥 Biggest Anomaly

!
Delhi's PM10 Spike: While Delhi's AQI (US scale) reads 82 (Moderate), the Indian CPCB scale places it at 148–155 with PM10 at a staggering 176 µg/m³. This is 4× above safe limits for PM10. The gap between US and India AQI scales masks the severity of Delhi's coarse particle pollution — a critical anomaly for health planning.
!
Annual AQI Worsening Trend: Despite June 2026 readings appearing "moderate," Delhi's 2026 annual AQI (177) has worsened 5.3% vs 2025 (179 avg). In 2026, 0% of 159 days have met WHO safe air quality limits — a deeply concerning long-term trajectory.

🔬 Most Surprising Observation

★
Kolkata > Delhi in June: Counterintuitively, Kolkata (AQI 133) is MORE polluted than Delhi (AQI 82) in June 2026 — reversing the common perception. This is unusual: Delhi typically dominates India's pollution rankings. The extreme humidity (84%) in Kolkata's pre-monsoon period is trapping pollutants near the surface, creating a temporary inversion effect that makes it the most polluted major metro right now.

📊 Executive Summary

📌
Analysis of 7 major Indian metros in June 2026 reveals average AQI of 76 (Moderate). All cities exceed WHO's PM2.5 guideline of 5 µg/m³. Bangalore is India's cleanest metro; Kolkata is currently most polluted. Delhi faces a paradox: "moderate" US AQI but critically high CPCB AQI and dangerous PM10 levels. Water quality across all cities remains poor with TDS 400–800 mg/L in Delhi, far above the WHO desirable limit of 300 mg/L, with significant impacts on skin and hair health.
