📰 Concept: Clickbait & Headline Manipulation

Headlines are the most powerful words in media. Studies show 59% of people share articles based on the headline alone without reading the full piece. Publishers know this, so they craft headlines to maximise clicks, often misrepresenting the actual story inside.

Everyday impact: When you scroll your feed, your emotional reaction to a headline shapes your view of the world even if you never open the article.

💬 Concept: Emotional Manipulation in Media

Beyond facts, modern media especially social media works by engineering emotional states. Fear, outrage, and awe are the three most viral emotions. Content creators know that if you feel strongly, you are more likely to share, comment, and engage. The emotion itself becomes the message.

Everyday impact: You may feel anxious, angry, or inspired after scrolling not because reality changed, but because algorithms fed you content optimised for emotional activation.
