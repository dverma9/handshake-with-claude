📄 Meeting Summary

The leadership team reviewed Q2 performance, which showed 12% revenue growth — below the committed 18% target. The shortfall is driven primarily by 5 enterprise deals slipping due to procurement delays and security review requests. While marketing signals are positive (34% traffic increase), conversion rates remain below expectations. A critical engineering bottleneck was identified around reporting performance and dashboard loading times, affecting both customer support ticket volume and live sales conversations. The team deferred hiring decisions and the customer conference budget pending further data, and agreed to avoid any public commitments on the August analytics dashboard release until a milestone review is completed.
