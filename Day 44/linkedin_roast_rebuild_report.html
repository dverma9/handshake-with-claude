Day 4 — Comment on 5 posts
The value comment formula

Find 5 posts by CTOs, engineering leads, or startup founders in your target industries. Apply this formula to each comment:

Agree — validate their point genuinely (1 sentence)

Add insight — share a specific experience you've had that adds to it (2 sentences)

Ask a question — end with a question that invites them to respond

Example: "Completely agree on the sprint planning problem. In my experience leading Healthcare IT teams, the biggest issue was scope creep disguised as 'minor' changes — we solved it by locking scope 72 hours before sprint start. Have you tried any fixed-scope windows in your team?"

Day 5 — Post #2
Lessons-learned / hot-take post

Draft — customise with your own experience

Hot take: Most software projects don't fail because of the technology.

They fail because nobody is actually managing the humans.

In 15 years and 7 industries, I've seen the same pattern:
→ Great developers. Unclear scope. No one owning stakeholder expectations.
→ Result: 6-month project delivered in 14 months, 2x budget.

The technical stack is rarely the problem. Kafka doesn't cause delays. People mismanaging Kafka does.

The most underrated skill in engineering is client communication. Not coding. Not architecture. Telling a CTO — clearly, calmly, early — that the deadline is at risk.

That's what I do.
