Before the rebuild, your profile was invisible to clients and unconvincing to anyone who stumbled on it. Every section told people what you are — not what you do for them. After the rebuild, your profile opens with a client problem, closes with a clear CTA, is packed with the right keywords for search, and every experience bullet leads with impact. A client who visits now understands your value in under 10 seconds.
One condition on the 86: this assumes you fill in the bracketed numbers in the About section and experience bullets with real figures. Without those numbers it sits closer to 74. The fastest single action to close that gap: spend 20 minutes writing down your real team sizes, project counts, and domain examples — then paste them in.
