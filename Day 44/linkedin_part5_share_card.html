Top 3 mistakes I was making
1
My headline opened with "Immediate Joiner" — the single fastest way to signal desperation and devalue yourself before a client reads a single word.
2
My About section had 500 words and zero proof. "Strengthened client partnerships" with no client name, no number, no result. Buzzwords where evidence should be.
3
Every experience bullet was a job duty, not an achievement. 15 years of engineering leadership — and not one number anywhere on the page.
The #1 thing that made the biggest difference
Rewriting the first 2 lines of my About section to open with a client problem instead of my job title. Those 2 lines are the only text visible before "see more" — and they were completely wasted. The new version made 3 people message me within a week.
