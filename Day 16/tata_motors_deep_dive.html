Company Overview

Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd (TMPV) is the passenger mobility arm of the Tata Group, emerging as a standalone listed entity after the strategic demerger of Tata Motors Group in October 2025. It houses three major businesses: (1) Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) — the UK-based premium & luxury auto brand contributing ~87% of TMPV's consolidated revenue, (2) Tata Passenger Vehicles — domestic ICE cars & SUVs (Nexon, Harrier, Punch, Safari), and (3) Tata Passenger Electric Mobility (TPEM) — India's EV market leader with 70%+ share.


FY25 (pre-demerger consolidated) delivered record revenues of ₹4.39L Cr, highest-ever PBT(bei) of ₹34.3K Cr, and net profit of ₹28.1K Cr. FY26 has been materially harder: JLR faced US tariff shocks (27.5% tariff on UK cars), a cyber incident in Q2, planned legacy Jaguar wind-down, and China headwinds — pushing FY26 revenues to ₹3.36L Cr (down ~8.3% YoY) and EBIT margins to 1.1%.

FY26 Headwinds — What Hurt Balance Sheet
  • 🔴 US Tariff Shock: 27.5% tariff on UK-produced vehicles from early FY26 materially impacted JLR margins and FCF. Partially resolved (UK-US deal: 10% effective Jun 30, 2025; EU: 15% via EU-US deal).
  • 🔴 Cyber Incident (Q2 FY26): A cyber attack on JLR led to production stoppages, impacting Q2 revenues by £1bn+ and deepening FCF outflows.
  • 🟡 Legacy Jaguar Wind-Down: Planned discontinuation of ICE Jaguar models ahead of all-electric relaunch. Created volume gap in FY26 — temporary, but earnings hit real.
  • 🟡 China Headwinds: Luxury tax and intense local competition squeezed JLR's profitability in China, a historically high-margin market.

Key Return Metrics Interpretation
  • 📊 FY24–FY25 ROE >28%: This was a genuine operational achievement driven by JLR's margin recovery, elimination of net debt, and operating leverage across all segments — a multi-year turnaround story.
  • 📉 FY26 ROE compression: Due to one-time tariff shock, cyber incident at JLR, and legacy Jaguar wind-down — all largely transitory. Underlying EBIT from JLR returning in H2 FY26 (Q4 adjusted EBIT ~9.2%).
  • ✅ TMCV (CV business): CV standalone ROCE of 37.7% in FY25 is exceptionally strong, demonstrating Tata's moat in the Indian commercial vehicle market.

Where TMPV Leads
  • ✅Global Premium Exposure: JLR gives unmatched access to high-margin luxury segments not available to peers.

Management & Governance Notes
  • ✅ Strong Board: N. Chandrasekaran (Chairman), Shailesh Chandra (CEO), PB Balaji (CFO) — all seasoned leaders with clear communication on JLR challenges.
  • ✅ Tata Brand: Long-standing Tata Group governance culture — high standards on ESG, transparency, and corporate conduct.
  • ✅ ESG: Sustainalytics "Low Risk" ESG rating. Net-zero target by 2045 (Project Aalingana). 300 MW renewable energy; water-positive facility certified.
  • ⚠️ JLR Cyber Incident (Q2 FY26): A notable governance/risk management concern — cyber resilience at JLR needs to be tracked.
💪 Strengths (5)
  • 🌍 JLR's Luxury Franchise: Jaguar Land Rover is a globally recognised premium/luxury brand with strong pricing power and margin potential when volumes recover. FY24–FY25 proved the capability with 8.5% EBIT margins and net debt elimination.
  • ⚡ India EV Market Leader: ~70% domestic EV share with Nexon EV, Tiago EV, Punch EV, and upcoming Sierra EV. Structural advantage as India's EV penetration grows from ~3% toward 30%+ over the next decade.
  • 🏗️ Post-Demerger Focus: Splitting CV and PV creates management bandwidth to execute strategies independently — better capital allocation, targeted investor appeal, and cleaner earnings narratives.
  • 💰 Tariff Tailwind: UK-US trade deal reduces tariffs on JLR UK-produced vehicles from 27.5% to 10% effective June 30, 2025. EU-US deal further reduces EU-produced vehicle tariffs from 27.5% to 15%. Meaningful margin recovery catalyst for FY27.
  • 📊 Attractive Relative Valuation: P/B of ~1.3x and P/Sales of ~0.43x are well below sector peers, offering potential mean-reversion value if earnings normalise toward FY25 levels.
⚠️ Watch-Points (5)
  • 🔴 JLR Earnings Cliff in FY26: EBIT margin collapsed from 8.5% (FY25) to 0.7% (FY26). JLR swung to a £(244)mn loss after tax. FCF was materially negative. Recovery depends on new Jaguar EV ramp, tariff normalization, and China stabilisation.
  • 🔴 JLR Concentration Risk: ~87% of TMPV revenue and disproportionate profits come from JLR. Any continued JLR weakness (geopolitics, EV competition, China) has outsized consolidated impact.
  • 🟡 Domestic PV Margins Thin: Domestic PV EBIT margin is only 1.4% in FY26. EV operations remain loss-making (heavy investment phase). Profitability at scale needs to be demonstrated.
  • 🟡 Net Debt Reversal: TMPV returned to net debt (₹30,700 Cr) in FY26 after achieving net cash in FY25 — largely due to negative JLR FCF from production stoppages. Watch trajectory in FY27.
  • 🟡 Currency & Macro Risk: JLR revenues are in GBP/USD; costs are globally dispersed. GBP volatility, US trade policy, EU tariffs, and China macro are structural sensitivities that are hard to hedge fully.
