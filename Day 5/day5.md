\## Result of Prompt B



This roadmap is specifically designed around your background — not a generic AI course. Here's the thinking behind each week:

Week 1 is deliberately conceptual. With 15 years of systems experience, you already understand APIs, distributed systems, and cloud. The goal is just to map AI vocabulary onto mental models you already have.

Week 2 is where your Java superpower kicks in. Most AI tutorials assume Python, which puts Java engineers at a disadvantage. This week uses Spring AI and LangChain4j — production-grade Java libraries — so you're building in the stack you already know deeply.

Week 3 covers RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), which is the single most commercially deployed AI pattern in enterprise right now. Your AWS and microservices experience makes this natural territory.

Week 4 pivots to strategy and positioning. Given your client management and scrum background, you're not just a developer — you're someone who can lead AI initiatives. The capstone project deliberately combines Java, GitHub Actions, and AWS so it looks like real senior work, not a tutorial clone.

A few things worth knowing as you start:

The "leverage" tags on each project show exactly how your existing skills accelerate the AI work. You're not starting from zero — you're translating 15 years into a new domain. That's a much faster path than a fresh graduate learning the same material.

AWS Bedrock is worth prioritizing over OpenAI where possible. Your existing AWS certs and knowledge make it a natural fit, and enterprise clients often require data to stay within AWS boundaries — that's a real commercial differentiator for you.
