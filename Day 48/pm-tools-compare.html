Scoring methodology

Each tool is scored 1–10 per criterion. Scores are derived from a combination of official vendor documentation, third-party review sites (G2, Plaky, TaskRhino, monday.com blog, cloudwards.net), and analyst write-ups verified against primary sources. Where a single authoritative number exists (e.g., "3,000+ integrations" for Jira from multiple review sources), it is cited as sourced. Where scores reflect a synthesis of qualitative evidence (e.g., "ease of adoption"), the score is flagged EST.

Integration ecosystem

Jira's 3,000+ figure is cited by multiple review sites (Plaky, monday.com blog) referencing the Atlassian Marketplace. Asana's 200+ is the vendor's own widely cited figure. Monday.com's 200+ dev-tool integrations is from their monday dev marketing and review comparisons. Notion's 100+ native integrations is from the Confluence/Notion 2026 comparison on saascrmreview.com.

Security & compliance

All four tools have confirmed SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications per their trust centers (Asana: trustcenter.asana.com; Notion: notion.com/security; Monday: trust.monday.com; Jira/Atlassian: trust.atlassian.com). Monday.com holds the most certifications (SOC 1, SOC 2, ISO 27001/17/18/32/27701, GDPR, HIPAA, FedRAMP). Jira offers on-premises Data Center deployment, a significant differentiator for regulated industries. Notion lacks HIPAA and FedRAMP eligibility. Scores reflect breadth of certifications + deployment flexibility.

Admin controls & user management

Jira (Atlassian Access) offers SAML SSO, SCIM, enforced 2FA, and audit logs with organization-wide admin across all Atlassian products. Asana Enterprise adds SCIM user provisioning and de-provisioning per their official plan comparison. Monday.com Enterprise includes SSO, SCIM, DLP, BYOK, and Tenant-Level Encryption via the Guardian add-on (trust.monday.com). Notion gates SCIM and audit logs to Enterprise tier (custom pricing) — a notable weakness per stitchflow.com analysis.

Automation capabilities

Jira uses rule-based drag-and-drop automation with JQL-based triggers and Atlassian ecosystem automation. Asana's automation is well-regarded for task-centric workflows. Monday.com uses a sentence-builder automation interface with no-code triggers described as more accessible to non-technical users. Notion's automation is more limited natively; it depends on Zapier/Make for complex flows. Scores are EST as no single authoritative benchmark compares automation depth.

Scalability

Jira is the de facto standard for large enterprise dev orgs; its Data Center product supports on-premises deployments at 500+ users. Monday.com's Maximum Quantity Billing (MQB) introduced July–Oct 2025 adds cost risk at scale. Notion is noted to "become disorganized at enterprise scale without strict governance" (docsie.io). Asana's portfolio and workload features are designed for cross-team coordination. Scores are EST for Asana and Notion as peer-reviewed scalability benchmarks were not found.

Ease of adoption

Consensus across multiple review sources: Monday.com is rated most intuitive for non-technical staff; Asana has moderate complexity; Notion has the steepest learning curve due to its open-ended block structure; Jira is powerful but notoriously complex to configure, especially for non-engineering teams. Scores are EST synthesized from qualitative review consensus.

Pricing note

Enterprise pricing for all four tools requires contacting sales. Published rates: Jira Standard $7.91/user/mo, Premium $14.54/user/mo (monthly billing); actual TCO $20–30/user/mo with add-ons. Asana Business $24.99/user/mo. Monday.com Enterprise custom. Notion Business $20/user/mo; Enterprise custom ($18–25/user/mo estimated by stitchflow.com). Pricing is not a scored criterion per your selections but informs context.

Conflicts between sources

Monday.com's integration count varies by source (some cite 200+, others 250+). We used 200+ as the conservative figure. Jira's pricing is complicated by the July 2025 Maximum Quantity Billing change — total cost of ownership is higher than headline pricing suggests. Notion's Enterprise pricing is not public; the $18–25/user/mo figure is from stitchflow.com's SCIM analysis and may not reflect all scenarios.
