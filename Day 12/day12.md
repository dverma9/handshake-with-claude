Claude entirely changed my job search journey by helping out with:



\* ATS friendly Cover letter



\* Recruiter Outreach Email



\* Hiring Manager Email



\* LinkedIn Connection Request



\* Referral Request Message



\* Follow-Up Email (After 5 Days — No Response)



\* 30-Second Professional Introduction



\* Skill Gap Analysis



\* Top 10 best suited roles for my profile



\* Key Strengths Recruiters Will Notice



\* Recommended Learning Roadmap



\* Personal Brand Summary\*\*



\* Interview Talking Points







You must be thinking - Can't I do that all on my own? Yes, ofcourse, I can. But still, I never did it... The reason is that it would have taken a lot of time preparing this toolkit by myself, for a single opportunity. Whereas, in the journey of job search we need to apply to many opportunities. If we keep on investing too much time for a single opportunity, then we will lose many others.
